# Code of Conduct

CloudMonitor welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where participants represent the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Offer specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Welcome questions and differing perspectives; assume good intent while
  remaining accountable for the effect of your words and actions.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing personal information.
- Accept reasonable requests to stop disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation against people who report
concerns are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Contact a repository maintainer privately using a contact method they publish
on their GitHub profile. If no private channel is listed, open an issue asking
for a private contact channel **without including names, allegations, or other
sensitive details**. Do not post private evidence in public issues.

If a report involves a maintainer, contact another project maintainer where
possible. Reports should include the relevant context, links, and the outcome
you are seeking. Share only information needed to understand the concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality as far as
practicable, and consider context and severity. They may remove content, issue
a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant. Serious
or repeated misconduct may lead directly to removal.

A person affected by an enforcement decision may ask for reconsideration through
the same private channel, providing relevant new information. Maintainers with
a conflict of interest should step back from the review where possible.
